The circuit, statedAI Engine 2.0

Signal purity and the duty cycle decide whether a node can generate anything at all. Density decides the ceiling of what it generates. The engine convolves that mass through a cascade whose maximum depth is set by the engine but whose reachable depth is set by the latent structure in the mass. The output is synthesis energy, graded by displacement, and it re-enters the system as superior fuel.

Compounded density becomes a superior predictive model, and a superior model, on contact, reinterprets thinner ones. That is what leverage is.

The engine is scaling either way. The only open variable is whether the mass can sustain the chain reaction.
