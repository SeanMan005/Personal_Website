What the panels are for. Directed learning is honest match to the world, measured against a fixed yardstick so that flattening the selector cannot flatten the measuring stick along with it; the dashed line is the gap between what the interface rewards and what is true. Compute is population volume and variants sampled per tick — rate and breadth of search, never its direction. Record is genetic diversity against the reachable variation still left. Search efficiency is match gained per 1,000 variants, which is the number that separates compute from learning.

The binding constraint. The lowest of the three bars is what the system is actually limited by at that moment. Raising anything else is wasted. blind-compute pours out variants with the filter switched off and learns nothing; degraded-selector keeps the loop turning while the field relaxes onto the population's own mean; frozen-record holds a good match until you press s and shock the field, which is the difference between having moved and having learned.
